# sc1-shot2 — The Visitor · prompt (approved v3)

```
[STYLE]
Late-1980s realism, cold grey-green, low saturation, slightly soft texture, locked-off camera.
LIGHT: exactly one source per shot — the standing lamp beside the sofa, warm, low; the light falls
evenly on the doorway and the floor between them. No second source, no local highlight, no spot,
no beam, no flare or highlight in the eyes.

[PLACE]
A small, kept flat at night: one sofa with a worn cover, a coffee table, a television switched on
with the sound down, rain on the window glass. The floorboards are dry in this shot. The background
is exactly this room — no extra furniture, no extra doors, nothing added.

[FRAMING]
Medium shot, locked off: the Son from head to knees, standing in the doorway at the right of frame;
frame edge just above his head, lower edge at his knees. The room is visible behind him. The Father
is not in frame. Nothing and nobody leaves the frame during the shot.

[CHARACTERS]
The Son: strictly copy the uploaded asset, identical. A dark T-shirt and jeans, soaked through —
the fabric clings, its colour darkened by water; hair wet, water running down from his hair and
clothes and dripping onto the floorboards. A worn canvas bag on one shoulder. No wounds, no blood.
The wet on him is clear water, not blood, and does not glow.

[ACTION 0–3s]
0–1s: he stands still in the doorway, one hand on the door frame, water dripping from his sleeve.
1–3s: he looks into the room — a small turn of the head to the left, no step.

[ACTION 3–6s]
3–5s: he shifts his weight onto his back foot: he does not come in.
5–6s: he lowers his eyes to the floor in front of him and holds there.

[REVERSE LOCKS]
Only one bag: no second bag, no duplicate of him. No extra furniture, no second doorway. He must
not come in, must not sit, must not speak, must not wipe his face, must not shake off the water.
The bag must not slip off his shoulder, must not change hands, must not disappear. The camera must
not move.

[CAMERA]
Locked off, medium shot. The camera does not move: no push-in, no pull-back, no zoom, no pan, no
handheld drift, no cut, no change of angle.

[SOUND]
No music, no score, no melody; no subtitles, no watermark, no logo. No flies, no insects, no
dripping-tap sound, no wind noise, no electrical hum. Only: rain against the window, water
dripping from him onto the floorboards, the television murmur from off-frame with its sound down.
Sound and motion happen together.

[DIALOGUE]
none in this shot.
```

## Why this shot is written this way

- **Framing by visibility** ("head to knees", "frame edge just above his head"), not by label.
- **Wetness defined against blood** — the single most common misread for a soaked character.
- **The bag gets a standing rule** — bags vanish mid-shot in this model unless forbidden.
- **One source, with the ban** — otherwise the generator invents a second light for the doorway.
- **"He does not come in"** — the beat is a refusal to move; without it the model walks him in.

Approved as v3 after two takes: take 1 walked him into the room; take 2 lit him from two sides.
