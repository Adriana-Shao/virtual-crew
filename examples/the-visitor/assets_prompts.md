# The Visitor — asset prompt document (fictional example)

Version v1 · Source: `script.md` (fictional) + `shotlist.md` + `world_state.yaml`
Purpose: **generate the plates first, then write shot prompts.** Name each generated image after its
entry id (`A2_son_soaked.png`) and mark the entry done. Everything here is fictional.

## Global style band (paste verbatim into every prompt)

【风格】late-1980s realism, cold grey-green, low saturation, slightly soft texture, 16:9 — fixed for
the whole film.
【光线】one light source per image, even and uniform; no second source, no local highlight, no spot,
no beam, no flare in the eyes.
【通用禁止】no text, no watermark, no logo; no extra people or shadows; no modern objects (flat-screen
TVs, LED lamps, contemporary clothing, plastic bottles).

describes only the change and copies the uploaded identity plate. Variants exist only where the story
changes something — the Father has none, because he never changes in this scene.

# 2 · Locations — four directions each

The flat: one room, one layout, four camera positions. Write the layout note first, then the four views
against it, so a door cannot move when the camera turns around.

## The flat · night · interior

Layout (shared by all four):
- **North wall:** the entry door — dark wood, single panel, handle on the right, a mat inside
- **East wall:** the sofa (dark green, one only), the low table (old wood, one chipped teacup on it), the
  television (old, switched off)
- **South wall:** the open passage to the kitchen — no door, just a lintel, dark beyond
- **West wall:** one old wooden window, rain on the glass; a folding chair beneath it
- **Floor:** boards, darkened by water from the doorway inward
- **Light:** the floor lamp beside the sofa is the only source (warm, low)

### B1 The flat · north view (camera at the entry door, looking south)
【画面范围】the room seen from the door: the sofa and low table on the left of frame, the dark kitchen
passage at the centre, the window with rain at the right edge.
【固定事实】one sofa, one table, one television, one window, one passage; the only light is the floor
lamp beside the sofa.
【禁止出现】no second door or window; no ceiling light; no modern furniture; nobody in frame.

### B2 The flat · east view (camera at the sofa, looking west)
【画面范围】square on the west wall: the old wooden window, rain running on the glass, the folding chair
under it, wet floorboards catching the lamp light.
【固定事实】one window, one chair; rain is visible only as water on the glass and as the wet floor.
【禁止出现】no view of the street outside; no second window; no curtains other than the plain one; no
visible rain streaks inside the room.

### B3 The flat · south view (camera at the kitchen passage, looking north)
【画面范围】square on the north wall: the entry door inside, its handle on the right, the mat on the
floor, the water trail leading in from the threshold.
【固定事实】the door is single-panelled, dark wood, and closed; the mat sits inside it; the wet trail
runs from the threshold inward.
【禁止出现】the door must not be a double door, must not be white, must not have a fanlight; nothing
visible outside the door; no second door.

### B4 The flat · west view (camera at the window, looking east)
【画面范围】square on the east wall: the dark green sofa from the front, the low table with a single
chipped teacup, the old television switched off beyond it.
【固定事实】one sofa, one table, one teacup, one television; the television is off — its screen is dark.
【禁止出现】no second chair or sofa; no flat-screen television; no books or bottles on the table; the
screen must not be lit.

## Discipline

1. One subject per prompt. Never two people in one character plate; never a person in a prop plate.
2. Every prompt carries the same style band, verbatim. Don't add words of your own — that is what
   breaks the band.
3. Name generated images after their entry id, and mark the entry done in this file.
4. If an entry comes out wrong, report it in plain language; the entry is rewritten (v2) rather than
   patched by hand, so the plate set stays consistent.
5. Once a plate is locked, shot prompts **reference** it ("strictly copy the uploaded plate") and never
   re-describe the person.
