# Shot prompt skeleton

Copy, fill, delete nothing. Sections are plain text with bracketed headers — no markdown — because the prompt is pasted verbatim into a generator.

```
[STYLE]
<project style block, verbatim: era, palette, saturation, texture>
LIGHT: exactly one source per shot, <source>, even and uniform across the frame; no second source,
no local highlight, no spot, no beam, no flare in the eyes.

[PLACE]
<scene name, time of day, interior/exterior>. <established facts that can drift: door form,
floor, furniture count>. <what the background is, and what it must not become>.

[FRAMING]
<what is visible, not the label: "head to knees in frame", "his face centred, frame edge just above
the chest">. <who is not in frame>. <what stays in frame throughout, nothing leaves the frame>.

[CHARACTERS]
<name>: strictly copy the uploaded asset, identical. <wardrobe paragraph, verbatim from the variant>.
<physical state: dry/soaked/injured>. <restraints and their standing rule>.
<second character, same shape>.

[ACTION 0–3s]
0–3s: <physical action, one clause per beat: what moves, what direction, what stays still>.

[ACTION 3–6s]
...

[REVERSE LOCKS]
<the named forbiddens for this shot: no second prop, no duplicated furniture, no extra hands,
no auto push-in, no cut, no crossing a wall, no second light source, no blood where there is water>.

[CAMERA]
<locked or moving, and if moving: start framing → speed → end framing → holds>. <the bans>.

[SOUND]
no music, no score, no melody; no subtitles, no watermark, no logo. <ambience bans>.
only: <the exact sounds allowed including dialogue>. <timing bound to actions>.

[DIALOGUE]
{line one}
{line two}
said once each, in this order, not repeated; mouth shapes match the words.
```

## Checklist before it leaves your hands

- [ ] every character's wardrobe pasted from the variant, not paraphrased
- [ ] no "same as the previous shot"
- [ ] framing given as visible content
- [ ] reverse locks named
- [ ] dialogue complete, in order, once each
- [ ] props: held by whom, in what condition, may not vanish
- [ ] hands assigned where two tasks compete
- [ ] light bound to one source, with the ban
- [ ] sound band present; off-screen voices have direction and timing
