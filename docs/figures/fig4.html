1 · Characters — two tiers, each tier writes only its own tier

The Son (19) — A2 · identity

【面部】narrow long face, narrow jaw, cheekbones a little high; thin straight brows; narrow single-lidded eyes set deep, short lashes; narrow straight nose with a small tip; thin lips, upper lip slightly short; narrow chin.

【面部独特特征】a very faint old scratch on the left cheek; a small mole inside the left wrist; pale skin with a greenish tint under the eyes.

【发型】dark short hair to above the ear, fine at the ends, one strand pushed forward over the forehead.

【体态】19, thin, narrow shoulders, slightly hunched; stands with the weight on the right foot.

【手与细节】long thin fingers, short nails, a small old callus at the base of the right thumb.

【禁止出现】don't change the hair or its colour; not muscular; no sexualised or suggestive pose; nobody else; no text or watermark; don't crop the body.

The Son (19) — A3 · soaked (V01)

【不变的部分】face, bone structure, hair and build strictly copy the uploaded identity plate — the same person, unchanged.

【变化的部分】a dark T-shirt and dark trousers, soaked through: the fabric clings, its colour darkened by water; hair wet and dripping onto his face; a worn canvas bag on one shoulder.

【禁止出现】the water must be clear — not blood, not red, not rust-coloured; no wounds, no blood anywhere; don't change the face.

Variants exist only where the story changes something — the Father has none, because he never changes in this scene.

2 · Locations — four directions each (N / E / S / W)

Layout note (shared by all four): North wall — the entry door, dark wood, single panel, handle on the right, a mat inside. East wall — the sofa (one only), the low table, the television switched off. South wall — the open passage to the kitchen. West wall — one old wooden window, rain on the glass. Floor — boards, darkened by water from the doorway inward. Light — the floor lamp beside the sofa is the only source.

B3 · south view (camera at the kitchen passage, looking north)

【画面范围】square on the north wall: the entry door inside, its handle on the right, the mat on the floor, the water trail leading in from the threshold.

【固定事实】the door is single-panelled, dark wood, and closed; the mat sits inside it; the wet trail runs from the threshold inward.

【禁止出现】the door must not be a double door, must not be white, must not have a fanlight; nothing visible outside the door; no second door.
